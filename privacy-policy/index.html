Privacy Policy

LuxHabitat Mumbai respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you use this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit a form we collect your name, mobile number, email address (optional), your message, the project you enquired about and basic technical data such as the page you were on and campaign source.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, share project details, arrange site visits and send relevant updates. We may share your details with the developer of the project you enquired about so they can assist you.

Communication consent

By submitting a form you authorise us and our partners to contact you via call, SMS, WhatsApp and email, even if your number is registered under DND/NDNC. You may opt out at any time by writing to hello@luxhabitatmumbai.com.

Data security & retention

We store your details securely and retain them only for as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. We never sell your personal data.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep the website working (for example, to unlock brochures you requested) and may use analytics cookies to improve our service.
